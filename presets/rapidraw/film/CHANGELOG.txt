RawSpeed creates separate 33^3 display-sRGB adapters at first use in its local
user cache. These are derivatives, not the original LUT files. The adapter
applies the inverse sRGB transfer function, then the Panasonic V-Log encoding,
then the original film LUT using trilinear interpolation. The original inputs
and outputs are Panasonic V-Log and sRGB, respectively. The generated adapters
remain CC BY-SA 4.0 and contain attribution, original source hash, and version.
The application also copies this license and a change notice to the cache.

This display-referred adaptation is not a reproduction of RapidRAW's original
scene-linear RAW pipeline, tone mapper replacement, or tetrahedral interpolation.
Clipped highlights and original scene exposure cannot be recovered from sRGB.

Modifier: RawSpeed contributors. Algorithm version: rawspeed-display-v1.
